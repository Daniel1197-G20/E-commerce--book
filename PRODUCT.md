# Product Specification: Chidi Okonkwo Digital eBook Platform

## 1. Product Vision & Mission
A premier, boutique digital publishing and eBook reading platform dedicated to high-craft literature, technology essays, and long-form volumes authored by Chidi Okonkwo. The platform bridges the physical intimacy of boutique books with modern, distraction-free digital reading and seamless instant commerce.

## 2. Core User Personas
- **The Deliberate Reader**: Values deep focus, thoughtful prose, and distraction-free digital reading without ads, clutter, or invasive tracking.
- **The Digital Collector**: Cares about DRM-free ownership, high-quality typography, multi-format accessibility (in-browser canvas, EPUB, and PDF), and building an aesthetic personal digital library.
- **The Author & Publisher Admin**: Needs a streamlined, high-control dashboard to manage digital catalog releases, edit metadata, upload manuscripts, inspect real-time sales transactions, and monitor reader engagement.

## 3. Core Feature Set & User Journeys
1. **Curated Storefront**: Editorial hero section, author quotes, featured showcase with 3 distinct view modes (Editorial Grid, 3D Cover Flow, and Tactile Bookshelf).
2. **Book Catalog & Discovery**: Real-time title/author/category filtering, instant quick-look modal preview, sample excerpts, and technical specifications.
3. **Immersive Web Reader**: In-browser reading canvas with customizable typography (Newsreader serif vs Plus Jakarta sans), size controls, warm color themes (Parchment, Warm Sepia, Midnight Slate), and persistent page progress.
4. **Frictionless Commerce**: Multi-item shopping cart with promo voucher support (`READ20`), instant Paystack checkout integration, and automated digital delivery into user library.
5. **Personal Reader Library**: Permanent DRM-free bookshelf with instant one-click web reader launching and offline EPUB/PDF downloads.
6. **Publisher Admin Portal**: Comprehensive management interface for catalog CRUD, pricing configuration, sales analytics, and transaction verification.

## 4. Guiding Design Principles
- **Editorial Warmth**: Every screen feels like opening a finely printed book on heavy woven paper.
- **Reading-First Hierarchy**: Content and typography lead; chrome and controls recede into frosted parchment surfaces.
- **Tactile Reality**: Subtle physical cues—spine creases, gentle elevation shadows, paper grain, and letterpress typography.
